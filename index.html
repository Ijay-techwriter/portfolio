How I work

Four steps, from the first look at your email to the day it ships.

Diagnose

I look at the email, the customer journey, the implementation, rendering and deliverability before recommending any changes.

Design

I create clear email layouts in Figma, with purposeful hierarchy, responsive structure, accessibility and a clear next action.

Build

I turn approved designs into responsive HTML email and set up campaigns or lifecycle flows in Klaviyo.

QA

I test rendering, responsiveness, links, personalisation, accessibility and dark mode before an email ships.
